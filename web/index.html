Pool fee surface

Each signer-manager is a separate contract with its own mutable fee and admin rules. Detected from the deployed interface, not assumed.

What this watches & why

PoX-5 (SIP-045) settles Bitcoin-staking rewards trustlessly, but the parameters around a locked bond stay mutable while stakers cannot exit: the protocol's pause-admin can fire an irreversible pause-rewards, the bond-admin can rotate who holds those keys, and every pool's signer-manager contract decides for itself whether — and by how much, and how fast — its fee can change.

BondWatch surfaces exactly those fields, read straight from the public Hiro node API and decoded in your browser. A generic "watch update-fees" rule is not enough: Fast Pool caps at 5% with a two-cycle delay, Xverse permits up to 100%, and Native Pool exposes no on-chain fee at all — so the monitor is per-manager-contract-aware.
